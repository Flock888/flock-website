Guided walks

Our founder Dominic offers guided walks to see the Flock birds. Hear the stories behind them, how they were made, and what's next for the project.

All funds raised from the walks go towards painting the next birds.

Message us on WhatsApp

Walks are private and informal, on foot and guided by Dominic. We don't arrange transport, ferries or meals. Hiking is at your own risk: wear proper shoes, bring water, and we may cancel for bad weather.
